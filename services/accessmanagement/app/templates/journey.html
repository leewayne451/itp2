Method: for a request at a step, look at past requests of the same type that spent at least as long at that step and see how much longer they took to finish (adding up typical step times instead when there are too few such cases). Durations are calendar time. A request that stalls suddenly cannot be foreseen, which is why not every late request is flagged early; it shows as stuck once it happens.
